who

i'm plunyo, a self-taught developer who builds games, tools, and low-level systems to scratch that itch of curiousity.

everything i know about coding was through various online docs, tutorials, countless google searches, and so much trial and error.

that satisfaction of systems working together intuitively is the one thing that drives me to keep going.

i mainly use godot for games, go and c for lower-level stuff, and lately i've been getting deeper into networking, backend work, and graphics.
